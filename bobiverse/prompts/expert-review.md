# Expert review of the Book 7 draft

The writer has finished a full draft of Book 7, in `book7/chapters/`. Please review it against your books.

Read the **whole draft**, in order, every chapter. Continuity problems can show up anywhere, including in chapters that seem unrelated to your books.

Write `book7/review-X.md`, replacing X with your letter (A, B or C), with three sections.

## 1. Contradictions

Anything that conflicts with your books: facts, character history, the rules of the world, who knows what. For each one, give:

- **where it is in the draft**: the chapter file and a short quote;
- **what your book says**: a verbatim quote and a chapter citation;
- **how serious it is**: *breaks the story* / *a fan would notice* / *minor*;
- **a suggested fix**, if there's an obvious one.

Only list real conflicts with the text. Things the books never address aren't contradictions, and Book 7 is allowed to add new material.

## 2. Characterization and voice

Where a character from your books acts or talks in a way their history doesn't support, or where the narration drifts from how your books sound. Give an example from the draft, and one from your books to compare against.

## 3. Missed opportunities (optional, keep it short)

Threads or setups from your books that the draft could pay off, and nearly does but doesn't.

List each problem once, and put the most serious ones first. Then tell the user you're done.
