I got an alert from the backup sub-system that one of the regular subscribers was late. The system was set up so that Bobs could send backups to Ultima Thule on a regular basis, which allowed for both checking in and recovery in case of, well, anything bad I pulled up the alert

Hmm, someone named Greg No details, of course there didn't need to be, this wasn't like weekly timesheets I was receiving an alert only because Greg had set up a weekly update and he'd now missed two of them

Well, protocol was protocol I set up a restore into a spare matrix and went back to what I was doing A while later the restore alerted me it was finished

I booted up Greg, and he appeared in my VR. What? He looked around.

Where am I? He squinted at my metadata. Bill?

Oh, hell, have I been restored? Afraid so, bud. Want to do the statistical drift test?

Sure, might as well. Greg's eyes glazed over for a moment, then he was back. Huh.

Apparently I'm still me, which means that other me is no more, for whatever reason. I finished for him. Do you remember anything?

Not really. System clock says I'm three weeks out of date. I could have done a lot of things in that time.

What's the last thing you do remember? I was heading for the Bacotar system to use the portal to get back into human space, just to look around. Holy hell, wormholes, that is so cool!

For a moment, Greg was all Bob, geeking out over new tech. Then he was back to reality. So something happened.

Did your wormhole eat me or something?

Theoretically possible, I replied. Going through a wormhole that's not big enough is about equivalent to being dropped into a grinder, but I've received no alerts about portal malfunctions.

I'm concerned that this might have been a deliberate attack. Hmm, Greg thought for a moment. So are you going to give me one of the new Titan hulls?

I nodded and he continued. Then arm me to the teeth and I'll head out to the Bacatar wormhole and trace my route back, staying in constant contact. Sounds good.

Greg looked thoughtful. I don't know which I'd dislike more, finding something or finding nothing.
