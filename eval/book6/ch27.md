As soon as we were back on BobNet, Goku and I connected up with Bill and popped into his VR. Hey, Bill, this is getting to be a habit.

It's always interesting, though, when you two show up. Some of the alternatives I've had to deal with...

Bill gestured to lazy boys. Park your butts and tell Uncle Bill all about it.

Okay, that's creepy, Goku observed. Don't do That, Bill laughed easily, but I gave Goku the silent side-eye.

He definitely went odd like that on occasion.

I pulled up our images and scans and laid them out in floating windows. Bill sat forward and pulled them over one at a time with a finger gesture, then examined each one intently.

He went several whole seconds without uttering a word. Finally, he sat back.

This ship is definitely not standard tech, even allowing for the PGF being more advanced than we are. This section here, he pulled up one of the scans

and Gestured, has a certain negative energy generator vibe, and a big one, although it's hard to tell with all the damage. Something really did a number on that ship. Yeah, about that, I replied.

Why would they turn off their surge drive? They would have had to, wouldn't they?

I don't see how they could be impacted like this otherwise. Hmm, yeah.

Bill cocked his head, looking through the video window rather than at it. That negative energy generator makes me think.

He gazed at the display silently for a few mils before continuing. One of the The Alcubierre models, actually most of them, would require you to turn off any sublight drive before activating the Alcubierre drive.

It's like a one in a billion chance of a rock showing up just at that moment, but it's not impossible.

Maybe they were attacked by someone? Goku said. Bill shook his head.

It just doesn't look right. No indication of explosives being used. The edges are torn, but there's no burning or melting or even the kind of bulging you'd get from a pressure wave.

And if someone was using kinetic weapons, I'd expect them to hit more than once. So, just random bad luck. Bill shrugged and gave me a lopsided smile.

Shit happens. Pretty sure it's a law. Are we wasting our time hauling it back to you?

Oh, hell no. I will go over it physically with a fine-toothed comb. Or a bunch of roamers.

Bring it on in, boys. We grinned at each other. Even Goku felt the nerding.

Oh, by the way, I added, the number of satellites you gave us was way overkill. It's not actually all that hard to force open the other end of the wormhole. Just slow.

Bill nodded. Good to know. Can I have my satellites back?

Hell no, I replied. This feels like something we should keep for just in case. Just in case what?

I gave him the Spock eyebrow. You're kidding, right? He laughed.

I get it. Like I said, shit happens. Goku and I replied in unison, it's the law.
