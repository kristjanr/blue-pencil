Scanning for planets with the telescope, I slid smoothly into the stellar system. I wasn't close enough yet for a SUDDAR sweep, even the long-range version, but at this stage, all I needed was a couple of hits for planetary objects to establish the orbital plane, then I could go in close and get a detailed survey. I waved, the holotank view closed and settled back into my lazy boy.

Guppy would alert me if the automated systems detected anything. Jeeves delivered a coffee, and all was right with the universe. I was happy enough to be out of meatspace and back to von Neumann-ing.

Humanity had just gotten too high maintenance over the last century, and the Bobiverse in general wasn't much better. I'd been around for the battle for Earth, and I'd been way too involved in the Starfleet War. Enough was enough.

The Elder Bobs could have all that and welcome to it. A status window popped up with the header, SUDDAR Detection, but there was no detail yet. Odd.

It was almost as if there was a... What the hell was that? Something incoming.

Guppy, get us out of... The ship shook with a sudden, violent impact. Alert.

Controller Replicant Offline. Surge Drive Offline. Requirements for Self-Destruct Protocol have been met.

Anti-matter containment disengaged.
