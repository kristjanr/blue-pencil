Will was on his patio, settled comfortably into a padded deck chair when I walked up. He looked up, said, hey, Bob, have a seat, then went back to his floating window.

I grabbed a chair, then took a moment to be amazed at the current technology. We were in real, the best reality, even in Manny's.

In fact, VR was taking a real hit in popularity, except for moots and other such occasions that required a bunch of widely separated people to come together. What had been gradually added over the last 10 years was AR, and with the Huey headsets, even BIOS could use AR to participate in the mashup. Oh, there were still glitches, but several companies were working on complete integrated solutions.

Will swiped the window to one side and looked up at me. We got a lot of suggestions about resettlement, not all reasonable. but many that we can't just dismiss.

I smiled. Give me an unreasonable one just for fun. Will chuckled.

Invade Romulus, kick out faith, allow ever-onward members to settle back into their lives. Well, it's not completely Bob. Sorry.

I grinned at him. But it does have the benefit of simplicity.

Will rolled his eyes. The most popular one, based on the number of suggestions and variations, is to settle in the Fargo, treating it as just another megastructure.

I looked up at the virtual blue sky, the virtual sun, and around at the very real-looking trees and low buildings dotting the landscape.

I can see that. So can I. And if it wins the voting, I'll be happy to hand it over to them.

You wouldn't stay on? Will shook his head. I don't want to get roped back into being a colony administrator

or advisor or local Bob or whatever you want to call it. I really, really have had it with running things.

I hear you. Kind of a been-there-done-that feeling. Time to move on.

Will looked up at me with an arched eyebrow. You're thinking of leaving?

No, no, I was referring to my time on Eden. Done that, got the t-shirt, no desire to go back. Ah, Will nodded, and idly reached over to pull his window back into his field of view.

Most of the rest is variations on join this colony, join that megastructure project, but there's this one. Will spun the window around for me, tapped an entry. This item was distilled down from a couple dozen variations.

Re-colonize Earth. Really? I frowned.

I thought colonization was what they were generally trying to avoid. Strangely, resettling Earth comes across as different psychologically, even though none of these people has ever seen it outside of history vids. I can see that.

I thought for a moment. Think Charles would go for it? I asked.

His response boiled down to, Hell, yes. I chuckled.

I guess there'd be no point to what he's doing, rebuilding all of Earth's ecosystems, if he wasn't prepared to consider settlers. Hmm. So, we're going to go through a few elimination rounds.

I'll bet real money, though, that the Earth resettlement option has a chance. No bet, I said.
