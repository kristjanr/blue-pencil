I pushed the button on the air horn, resulting in the inevitable blat, followed by the inevitable booing. It was more subdued than usual, though.

People knew there were serious events afoot, and it was taking some of the air out of the usual party atmosphere. Most of you know by now that someone is attacking all the colonies. The human navies are fighting back, but they're outnumbered.

I know this isn't everyone's favorite situation, but... I'm asking for volunteers to help defend the human settlements. I held up a hand to forestall the angry roar that I could feel building.

Yes, I know, we swore off being cannon fodder after the battle for Earth. And yes, the bio's reaction to the Starfleet war has undoubtedly left a bad taste in everyone's mouths. Nevertheless, people are dying.

Hell, we'll do that afterward even if it doesn't get destroyed. If you don't want to get directly involved but have an inventory of busters, contribute them. If you are in a position to do some printing, make more.

We do have some unclaimed Titan-class vessels sitting around as well. We could use drivers for those.

Yeah, but why? So the BIOS can crap on us all over again? It was a different speaker, but the same general tone.

I took a deep breath before replying, not wanting to come off sounding annoyed.

I can only point out that the reason for doing the right thing isn't to earn other people's good opinion, it's for your own.

This is the right thing. Unless you've drifted a whole lot, you know that you'll feel bad if you walk away. This statement got me a whole lot of angry muttering.
