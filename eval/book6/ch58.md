I wanted to talk to Teresa about her diplomacy efforts with the PAV. Specifically, was she working on behalf of the UFS or the Quinlan's? And was there even a conflict between those two options?

It hadn't been an issue worth pursuing when we started this project, but given the Pav tendency to kick ass in battle, I had decided it could be a concern down the road.

I pinged her and received an acknowledgement and an address, so I popped over. Out of courtesy, I adopted a Quinlan form. When I arrived, though, I found Theresa in human form, dressed in full ballet garb, with a human instructor in the middle of adjusting her stance.

I glanced around to verify my first impression. This was a full-on dance studio. And there were other people present, also with instructors, all doing...

ballet things. The instructor looked askance at me, a slight smile on her face.

We don't usually do cross-species instruction, she observed. But if we can find you a tutu? I held up my paw to stop her and changed into my default Bob form.

Sorry, I was... I stopped, uncertain if the instructor even knew Teresa's true nature. They both laughed, and Theresa said,

It's okay, Bob. Barbara knows I'm really a small furry quadruped. Okay, that turns down the awkwardness a little bit, I replied.

Looking around again, I spied some chairs, with a few individuals seated, watching the proceedings.

Uh, this is VR, right?

Yes, Barbara replied. I like to keep the environment as real-life adjacent as possible, though, including turned ankles if you mess up your jeté. I tilted my head, not quite certain how to ask.

Are you? I am post-life, Bob. As are many of my students.

When you find yourself with all the time in the world, it's natural to take up some of the things you wanted to try in life. But since the advent of Huey's and the VR interface, we've started enrolling bios for remote instruction. She smiled.

It has greatly increased my student base. Barbara's studio is one of the premier ballet schools, Theresa added. And with that in mind, you're cutting into my hour.

Go. Sit. Down.

Yes, I'm, I said, and went to park myself on the available chairs. I watched with half my attention while I mused on the new synergies. Between VR, AR, Hueys, and the VR interface for humans, reality had become quite fluid.

In fact, other than the ability of digitals to frame jack, there was very little to distinguish us from bios, at least in public spaces. I wondered if this would increase acceptance of replication in the long term, assuming medicine didn't just outright crack immortality. Meanwhile, Theresa's session appeared to be coming to an end.

She walked over, towel draped over her shoulders. Quite pointlessly, I thought to myself, people didn't sweat in VR. Except Theresa was sweating.

I could see a distinct sheen on her face and neck. She must have correctly analyzed my facial expression. Barbara believes in full verisimilitude, Bob, including sweat, to remind you that you are indeed exercising.

It wouldn't do to have a client try something in real only to have a heart attack because their meat body couldn't physically handle it. Huh, that's dedication. I stood as Theresa headed for the front of the studio.

So do you have to shower and change too? Theresa laughed. No, there's a limit to what even Barbara will try to enforce.

You wanted to talk to me. That was Theresa's well-known directness in operation, only the fact that she was smiling kept it from feeling dismissive.

Um, how about we talk over lunch? Sure, VR or Manny. Let's grab a couple of Mannys on Vulcan and get burgers.

We agreed on an address, transferred our consciousnesses, and in moments I was stepping out of a Manny pod in the floating city of Landing, on Vulcan. Teresa came out a few moments later looking exactly like Teresa. The human version, that is.

Wow, the feature matching has really come a long way, I commented. That could easily be a custom Manny. You look good too, Teresa said and took my arm.

I had a momentary mental image of Garfield pointing and making some ribbled comment and I shoved the thought down. It was none of his business anyway. We settled on a burger joint not too far from Manny Park Remote Services.

Original Bob had been a burger and fries kind of guy and replicative drift hadn't affected that preference at least as far down as Herschel and Neil. Theresa had quickly picked up a taste for land meat, as she called it, from frequent lunches with me. Of course, it was VEET these days, but I had done a taste test once at one of those marketing booths, and you literally could not tell the difference.

We sat at a table with a magnificent view through the glass X dome. The surface of Vulcan had by now reverted almost completely to primeval jungle as humanity took to the air. The wildlife was abundant and riotous and probably loud as hell if you were outside.

We gave our orders to the table, then I said, So, uh, ballet?

Theresa returned my look with a smile.

Remember we had a conversation about species differences a few years ago? I nodded and she continued.

My wanting to learn human ballet is really no different from you wanting to enjoy swimming in Quinlan form. It's a kind of liberation from the limits of your natural physiology.

I thought of Howard's Amusement Park, which had just opened to record-level crowds and smiled. Uh-huh. That and flying.

I'll want to try that sometime as well. Theresa paused. Although human dance often feels like one is defying gravity.

Have you discovered any earth-shaking secrets about the Pav, though? They call humans nosies. I snorted.

Yeah, I was there. Anything else? They're dichromatic.

Oh. Well, that's not really earth-shattering. Bridget will be interested, though.

And they are fierce warriors, which is what I suspect you wanted to talk to me about. I grinned at her. You got me.

Theresa, they're mopping the floor with Medeiros any time they go up against him,

numbered. One of our military guys described their style as organized berserkers, which is an oxymoron to humans.

And your point is? My point is to wonder how your diplomacy is leaning. Are you trying to bring

them into the UFS? Or are we going to find ourselves facing a Quinn-Pav alliance at some point? Teresa

cocked her head. You are a part of this diplomatic mission. Not really.

I mean, I'm part-time at best. You're going at it with your usual maniacal focus. I know for a fact you've had meetings that I haven't

been privy to. So I need to know. Are you playing a third hand here?

Theresa sighed and placed her hand on mine across the table. Bob, please. I know who my friends are.

I wouldn't betray you like that. What I am doing is convincing the Pav that they are repaying their debt to humanity in full. You were there when they explained why that was a sore point as well.

I think you'll find Pav human relations thawing significantly in the future. She paused and smiled widely. Just don't, as you say, piss them off.

Our food picked that moment to arrive and we were both distracted by the carnivore-friendly odor. Bronto burger with cheese. Yum.

So much better than fish parts, I muttered around a mouthful. Mm, squiz, Theresa responded. I gagged.
