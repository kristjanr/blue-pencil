Bob, aboard the Fargo, October 2347. The days of physical ballots and traveling to the polling station were long over, but people were still social animals and loved to argue. Somehow, by osmosis, a decision had been collectively made to gather at the centers of the various towns that Will had set up and watch the results come in.

The township that my manny was stationed in was named Brooklyn. It had a population of around 2,500 humans, and a lot of them were gathered in the central square at the moment, walking or sitting under trees or on benches. Some had AR windows open, presumably tracking the vote, although many had privacy active, so all I could see was a gray frame floating in the air

I checked the status of the referendum in my heads up It wasn't quite official yet, but the numbers were pretty clear If this had been a political election, I think the losers would have conceded by now

The apparent winner was recolonize Earth Will had pretty much called it After all, if you were going to set up from scratch on a planet, why not one that had guaranteed compatible biology?

Charles and crew had been cutthroat about ensuring that no alien imports took hold on earth and an aggressive seeding and breeding program meant that very little of the planet was still barren. Even areas like the Sahara had been remediated thanks to weather control. The only alternative that had even come close in votes had been the option to live in the Fargo indefinitely.

And in my opinion, that would have been short-sighted. The Fargo was a nice size, but didn't have the space to handle an expanding population indefinitely. I sent off a quick text to Charles and got a response within moments.

Hey, Bob, Bill's over here right now talking to me. Want to pop over? Well, why not?

I looked around and spotted an empty bench off in a corner. I ordered the manny to go sit down and ping me if anyone tried to interact, then transferred to Charles' VR. Charles, Bill, and Will were seated around a campfire in folding canvas chairs.

It appeared to be early evening, with the sun just about to set and a slight chill in the air. Will had a stick with a marshmallow at the end of it, but didn't appear to be trying especially hard to roast it. I snickered, more in surprise at the scene than anything, then looked around, just as another chair appeared for me.

bit of a departure from your normal style, Charles. Don't you usually have an office? I thought this would be appropriate for the discussion at hand.

He replied, Will got here just before you. I guess he saw the voting results too. I had to park my mani, I explained to no one in particular.

Charles said to the group, apparently continuing a conversation, so as far as the question of whether I'd be okay with our descendants recolonizing, of course I would. We haven't been rebuilding Earth's ecosystems just to turn it into a museum piece. Most of the climate zones have stabilized and we've got full-grown ecosystems now.

Will nodded. It would be nice to see the planet repopulated and thriving again. It would make the

whole space colonization thing feel more like expansion rather than exile. There were more quiet nods around the fire.

Then Bill said, what about the other things you've been working on, Charles? Like the moon, Mars, and so on.

They aren't quite ready yet, Bill. Luna's close. Mars, not so much.

But when they are, why not? Same comment applies, he pointed upward.

That's an accurate representation of what the moon looks like from Earth right now. We all looked up.

The full moon hung over us, but unlike the moon I remembered from my pre-replicant days this one had colors that weren't variations on gray. White clouds, blue seas, brown land, even some green here and there.

It could almost be mistaken for a view of Earth. Except, interestingly, the seas were still in the same general shape as the old man-in-the-moon face. What's making up the green?

I asked. Lichen, hardy grasses, other hard-to-kill plants? Charles replied.

Some limited forestation. I've been taking advantage of Bill's experience as terraforming Ragnarok. How soon could you be ready for us?

I've been refining that list ever since we started the original Exodus. We can put a complete package together in a few weeks. If I can get some help from your local auto factories.
