I was going over my notes on the warp drive when I got a ping from Calvin.

We hadn't yet implemented VR through the Outlaw comm system, so he popped up in a video window when I accepted the call. Goku sat slightly to one side, nursing a coffee.

Hey, Bill, just testing out the new comm system, Calvin said. Seems to work well. How's it going with that Hulk we brought you?

I grinned at him. Oh, pretty good. We got some results.

Calvin gave me the hairy eyeball. I recognized the tone. Something big has happened.

Give. I laughed and gave them a summary of our work with the warp drive. When I was done, they both looked distinctly boggled.

Goku was the first to recover.

So, you're going to use this to go after Nemesis and move it? Is that the plan? Well, plan is a strong word.

At this point, it's more like a shared fantasy. We have a lot of testing to do before we can raise this to the level of a plan. I shrugged.

For instance, can we use pusher plates to move a black hole? Will frame dragging around the black hole affect things? How close can we place them without them getting sucked in?

And although this isn't a serious option, but everyone is curious, what happens if you try to push a black hole through a wormhole? Huh, Calvin replied. So are you going to run some tests?

Ah, sure, buddy. All we need is a black hole. You got any?

The closest one I know of is about 1,500 light years away and no wormhole shortcuts available. Instead of replying, Calvin and Goku turned and looked at each other. As Calvin had recognized my tone, I recognized that look.

You guys have a black hole, don't you? Calvin laughed. Well, have is a strong word, but we know where you can find one.

Cool, how big? We figure about 2.5 solar masses given the movement we recorded before we retreated with alacrity. Well, that's not bad, I mused.

That'd be about 330 billion pusher plates. It's way down from 26.4 quintillion, but still not something I could whip together in my lab. Yeah,

I think the general minimum for a stellar-mass black hole is 2.1 sols, right? Theoretically. Everything about this is theoretical, Bill?

Where'd you get 330 billion from anyway? Ah, based on the setup that Ick and Day used to send the two planets into the other's star. Do you really need that much capability?

Calvin asked. I mean, if you can generate a vector and it fits the calculated effect, isn't that enough? I thought about that.

He was right. I didn't need to accelerate the black hole at 50 G's to get it to a star. I just needed to make it wobble a bit.

If the wobble matched what the calculations said it should be, then that was confirmation. Based on that, I could probably get by with no more than a half million or so. Piece of cake.

I gave them a sickly smile. Calvin replied, well, it actually is. If you set an auto factory to making more auto factories, you can double your inventory every six months.

At that rate, it would take about five years to get a million of them. Then you start making plates.

Or, I held up a finger, I just got an offer from ANEC to use the Quinlan's manufacturing capability.

They are way ahead of us in this respect. After all, they were able to build a billion-mile-long topopolis in less than a generation.

So how long to produce enough, Calvin said. Don't know. I'll have to talk to ANEC.

I'll also have to allow for the fact that war production takes priority, but the black hole isn't going anywhere, right? Uh, what? I demanded.

The black hole isn't going anywhere, but the portal may not be there much longer, he looked at Calvin. Did we actually get around to calculating how long until the black hole eats the portal?

No. We may have to go back and check. Calvin looked at me.

Hey, if we push a survey drone through and leave it, can you connect to it using the outlaw system, then use it to monitor the black hole's movement?

Absolutely, and that'll give me the numbers I need. But maybe not the ones you want. Yeah, thanks, Calvin.

Just go set it up, okay? Hey, maybe leave a couple of backups first. Just in case.

You know? Calvin made a face. You are a real downer, Bill.
