I was a little surprised to find myself feeling butterflies. It had been more than two centuries since I'd first faced off against Major Ernesto Medeiros,

and although this version of him couldn't possibly have the memories of that specific encounter since I'd destroyed the version that was there, nevertheless, he seemed to know enough about it to have a real hate on for Bob Johansson in all his incarnations, including, and maybe especially, me.

Well, there was no point in delay. I pressed the transmit button. It would send a message out to all the colonies ensuring that Medeiros would at least hear it.

Whether he would react as hoped, well, that was another topic.

This is Bob Johansson calling Major Ernesto Medeiros. I bring an offer not of surrender, but peace. Your primary goal is not necessarily conquest of human—

And what would you know of my primary goal, Mr. Johansson? I stopped, taken aback.

That was quick. The response was also coming from the 82 Eridani system, according to my instruments. Not that I could necessarily read anything into that.

It would almost certainly be a red herring, probably relayed from somewhere else on an encrypted channel. Major, you haven't been the slightest bit coy about your goals. In fact, you've ranted about them at every opportunity.

This war is just a means to an end, to reinstate the Brazilian Empire. Tell me I'm wrong. It is a gross oversimplification, but not incorrect in the essentials.

Still, I see no reason to modify my tactics. The goal is the same, however you may choose to interpret it. Okay, well, interpret this, Major.

The Brazilian Empire has been reconstituted on Earth, governed by people of Brazilian descent. Right now.

There was silence on the other end for several seconds. Medeiros was as much a computer as I was, so that length of delay was significant. I decided there must be a background debate going on that I wasn't privy to.

And why should I believe you, Mr. Johansson? You don't have to, Major.

A representative of the Brazilian Empire will get in touch with you and you can verify for yourself. But meanwhile, you are fighting a war that is not authorized by your country. I suggest you suspend hostilities until you have checked out my claim.

Worst case, you've delayed a day or two. There was a low chuckle from the other end. Very well, Mr.

Johansson. I will take your suggestion under advisement, but do not delay. My patience is already strained.

Medeirosaut. I deactivated the scut and turned to my audience.

That went well. Usually, Garfield observed. We say that when it didn't.

Thanks, Captain Obvious. I glanced over at Bill. How are we doing on the actual Brazil?

Will's busier than an anteater at a company picnic. If ever he was going to replicate himself again, he'd do it now. We've got a government.

The palace is almost done. We've drafted laws and a constitution. It's all very exciting.

And no pushback? I didn't say that. A lot of other groups are apoplectic that Brazil is getting its original territory back.

They say it's like capitulating to terrorism. They say it's not fair. They say it's—I rolled my eyes.

Did you try explaining it? Sure, because that always works. Bill rolled his eyes back at me.

Will told them if they had a better way to stop the war right now, they should step forward. Otherwise, shut the hell up. I think that was what you call a last nerve moment.

Okay, I said, laughing. We may yet pull this off.
