My answering service dinged for the third time in an hour. I checked the caller ID.

Sure enough, the New Home Ministry of Internal Affairs. I didn't bother playing the message or reading the transcript. It was from one of Horst's secretary,

and would be artificially polite. I figured I'd get at least one more message from an underling before Horst came on himself.

Then I'd listen to the message. Bridget glanced up at me from her computer. You look like you just won the lottery.

Did he finally call? Not yet. I think I'm due one more

call from staff before he breaks down. The question is whether he'll still have a job at that point. Bridget laughed, the full-throated husky trill that always made my IQ drop.

She'd seen the video, no need for explanations. An hour later, I got a text from Will.

What did you do? I was taken slightly aback. I'd been hip-deep in final prep, planning for the Adventure Park opening and lost track of time.

I may have missed some event that Will was referring to. I did a quick scan of the news feeds. Ah, Rickman Horst had just gone on leave from his position as chancellor, citing health concerns.

Uh-huh. I pinged Will back, then popped into his VR. Hey, Will, seen the news, have you?

Will gave me a credible attempt at an evil eye, then broke down and grinned. There was never any doubt in my mind that this was your doing. I just haven't figured out how yet.

I grinned back at him. Well, you know that about two months ago, Horst strong-armed me into putting a visual identification on all Hueys and Mannys on New Home? Yeah, the infamous H.

He chuckled. That was great. And as far as I know, none of us talked about it publicly, per your request.

Gonna tell me why now? Uh-huh. About a month ago, the Stream Us Network just happened to host a Red Dwarf revival, all restored footage, with a ton of marketing fanfare to make sure people watched it.

Not sure where they got the financing. Sure, no idea, Will said, still grinning. Hey, who's telling this story?

Anyway, three days ago, Stream Us aired the Rimmer Experience episode. Two days ago, someone, again, no idea who, released a modified version with all the background Rimmer puppets replaced by Rickman Horst puppets. As I said it, I popped up a video window with the segment in question in mid-play.

Will's eyes became big as saucers, then he began to laugh uncontrollably, eventually sliding right off his chair. As the video came to a close, Will finally got the guffaws under control and levered himself up So, no idea who, he said, vainly attempting to wipe away the tears

Nope, and as has been shown through history, people in government can take anything except being laughed at I actually wasn't expecting a reaction for another day or two, and I'd have given even odds that it would be involuntary Go figure

I paused for a moment, then added Oh, and some enterprising soul, again, no idea who, started making and selling adhesive H's in the same style. They've become a fashion accessory for bios, and in the process completely obliterated the whole point of requiring them in the first place.

Will continued to chuckle. Some people never learn. He paused, then became more serious.

The thing is, Howard, I understand that it feels good to hand the bullies their collective ass once in a while, but there are consequences downrange for stuff like this. That's now one more colony that's going to oppose us on anything and everything. even if it will harm them.

I rubbed my forehead, unhappy both that the conversation had just gotten serious and that Will was harshing my buzz. I know, I know.

Not that New Holm has been in our cheering section in the past. They're probably still smarting from being outflanked financially by us on the Starfleet war

fallout. Will tried and failed to keep a straight face. With a small smile, he replied, oh, I know.

Every time I attend a council session, the New Holm rep manages to get in a dig at our unscrupulous and Untrustworthy Reputation. It's going to get worse now.

So, Will shrugged. Hey, listen, I don't have anything specific.

It's just a general feeling that making enemies isn't something you should do lightly. Let's hope I'm just being paranoid.

Sure, Will, I get it. I wouldn't say it out loud, but the feeling remained that it had been worth it.
