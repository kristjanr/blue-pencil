April 2348 Theresa and I were in a discussion with the PAV planetary governor, Pazila Bahir, when we received a high-priority text from Bill.

Medeiros is making an announcement. I jerked and Bahir glanced at me with a quizzical expression.

Medeiros, I said, some kind of public announcement. We should turn on the news.

The news? Bahir said in a puzzled tone. Oh, you mean the information feed?

She gestured to her assistant and a monitor came to life. On it, Medeiros' face was displayed with the Brazilian flag behind him. I noticed that his facial distortion was continuing to progress.

We were catching him mid-rant, apparently.

More than enough opportunity to do the rational, the right thing, yet you resist. Worse, you continue to consort with the execrable Bob Johansson. My patience is at an end.

All remaining colonies have one week to suspend defenses and surrender. If you do not comply, I will launch relativistic attacks against your home planet, which will result in 100% casualties. In addition, you will sever all ties with the Bobiverse, and you will hand over any and all related physical assets, including any actual Johansson ships and matrices.

I have arranged a small demonstration to make my point. That is all. His face faded, replaced with the flag.

Beheer gestured, and the monitor dimmed. Well, she said in an artificially light tone, that seems fairly unambiguous. Do you think any colonies will comply?

Sadly, yes, I said. Some will always choose the easy path. Beheer nodded thoughtfully, then straightened.

We cannot, of course, trust this Medeiros not to turn his eye in our direction once he has finished with the human colonies. I will speak to our commander-in-chief about a full-scale commitment. The humans will certainly appreciate it, Theresa said.
