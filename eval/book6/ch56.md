The deployment of the early warning systems went quickly. After all, that didn't really involve new tech, just a redeployment of existing tech for a new purpose. Within a couple of days, most of the remaining colonies had a shell of surveillance drones equipped with long-range SUDAR.

The colonies had to implement a cataloging system to identify all friendly equipment, but by the end of the first week, every colony could spot and identify any patrolling or incoming Madeira in plenty of time to react.

Of course, spotting them was one thing, and dealing with them was another.

Most systems were in the neighborhood of 5 to 8 light hours across, and even with 100G acceleration, it could still take up to 24 hours to reach points in the outer system.

Of about 25% of C, so it still took significant time to move limited defensive assets around the system.

I took a call a few days later from the Supreme Commander of the 82 Eridani Combined Naval Forces, Admiral Callum Stewart. Admiral Stewart somehow had managed to maintain a strong Scottish accent, a rarity almost two centuries after the exodus.

What's up, Admiral? Mr. Johansson.

We've managed to knock out three Medeiros incursions with minimal losses, which was hugely gratifying. However. I sighed.

It's just Will, please. And however is never a good way to start a sentence.

He's adapting. He has. He's obviously figured out our tactic.

Yesterday he made two feints to draw off our forces, then sent in a large force at 200 degrees anti-spinward. We were unable to bring our defenses around in time and ended up losing one of our space docks, along with considerable wasted munitions. Of course, I am hoping you can provide us with some assets to shore up our defenses.

This was not unexpected, which didn't make it any more palatable. And our supply of warp torpedoes was, well, not even really a supply yet.

More of a statement of principle.

Just the same, we'd started to enjoy some positive news, and it wouldn't do to pull the rug out on that so quickly. Despair was a very real danger in wartime, and surely is getting simply blown up. I will deploy some new tech to the 82 Eridani system immediately, Admiral.

Are you currently under attack? He's executing feints. Well, we think another attack is coming.

As soon as he feels he's scattered our defenses enough. Got it. Give me a few hours.

Understood. Out. I immediately popped over to the command center where Thor was tirelessly coordinating and commanding the colonial forces.

Well, the replicant forces, anyway. Many of the colony navies were still only grudgingly admitting our existence. I will.

Never good news when you show up. Just saying. I replied with a tired smile.

A lot of that sentiment going around, Thor. Listen, do we have any warp torpedo deliveries yet? Just received a hundred from the Quinlan auto factories.

They're in Epsilon Eridani waiting to be allocated. How will they be delivered? Self-guided.

We've set up a script for the AMI pilots. No reason to ship them as cargo when they have their own drive systems.

We just tell them where to go and who to report to. Even through wormhole portals? Well, we do have to get involved there.

Not just because of the complications involved in transiting. The Medeiri have destroyed the portal satellites and taken to blockading the portals. We've shrunk them so they can't send something through and Medeiros doesn't have the tech to force them open from his side.

But we have to force them open from our side to use them and the moment we do so, Medeiros starts sending missiles through. Did Bill say anything about flying a torpedo through a portal on warp drive? Uh-huh. He said, don't do that.

Wonderful. Do they have a surge drive? Not a big one, just good enough for local maneuvering, Thor smiled.

Impulse power. Okay, we'll have to use up some torpedoes to clear the area. Have them accelerate toward the portal on our side, timed with expanding the portal.

Don't give Medeiros time to spot them. As soon as they're on the other side, go FTL for a few mils, turn around, and target the Medeiri. Can we do that?

Sure. Thor made a summoning gesture and a bob walked over. This is Mick.

He's our warp torpedo script specialist. To the extent we have one, Mick interjected. It's been a theoretical pastime until now.

I quickly summarized my conversation with Thor. Mick nodded.

That's doable. It's well within the operational expectations for the units.

Also, and I'm kind of nerding out about this, ANEC improved the AMI software a lot. These babies are way smarter than our standard stuff.

Cool. Let's do this. 42 times light speed meant that all the normal periods of delay involved in going from A to B were effectively nullified, for small values of A to B anyway. From the collection point to the portal leading to 82 Eridani was a matter of minutes.

It took a lot longer actually to stand them off a few thousand kilometers and have them accelerate toward the portal while Thor built up the negative energy charge. The portal expanded and the missiles flew through. At least a half dozen were destroyed immediately after transiting, probably due to collisions.

At the same time a dozen Medeiri missiles entered our side. They were immediately dispatched by defensive forces with minimal losses. Thor closed the wormhole before Medeiros could follow up with more units, then pulled up monitor windows.

We can't track them while they're in warp, Mick explained, but we are getting a mirror of what the 82 Eridani defensive forces are seeing. He leaned forward and gestured to points on the display.

Medeiros' forces are approaching the inner system here, here, and here. Total about 60 units. We can't tell which ones are a feint, or if maybe there's another group waiting just out of range for us to commit.

So what's the plan? I asked. Simple and straightforward.

Blow them all up. No matter the details of his plan, these are real combat units and he'll suffer real losses. Works for me, I muttered.

I took a few moments to send an email to Admiral Stewart advising him to expect results within minutes. Mick expanded one of the windows.

This is a close-up of the portal area in 82 Eridani. There are eight Medeiri on patrol.

Shall I blow them up? How are you getting this telemetry? I asked. One of the warp torpedoes is sitting a light minute away doing a SUDAR scan. The Medeiri know it's there, but they can't get to it in less than an hour and a half, even at their max acceleration. Handy, I muttered. Then?

Take them out. Mick froze for a moment, presumably sending commands in frame jack, then one by one, the Medeiri units winked out.

And done, Mick said with obvious satisfaction. How long to get the remaining units to the inner system?

Thor asked. Six to eight minutes, Mick replied. I grinned.

We are about to hand Medeiros a good solid ass kicking. Thor chuckled, just like old times.

The warp torpedo tactics were simple. Proceed to within a light minute of a specified location, do a SUDAR sweep to pinpoint the target, micro jump to that location, explode. It was a strategy with no possible defense, at least with the technology available to Medeiros, and it worked exactly as advertised.

20 minutes after we opened the portal, the 82 Eridani inner system was clear, at least to the limits of our scans.

Stuart called me. Well, that was fantastic. Do you have a lot of those, whatever they are?

Can we have more? Warped torpedoes. Admiral, we have eight left in your system.

We'll leave them with you, along with an operating manual and some software. Our auto factories are working to churn out more, but it's a slow process.

Plans, but be aware that you can only produce one every three days with a standard autofactory. The problem isn't scale, it's the level of complexity for the negative energy generator. That's fine, we'll take what we can get, and if we can start knocking the enemy out before they even get close, we'll have the advantage.

We'll keep producing them at this end as well, Admiral. Let's hope we can build up some stock before Medeiros gets wise. Out.

I grimaced as the call ended and Thor said, that's the thing, isn't it? The moment Medeiros realizes the overall situation has shifted, he'll do something desperate. I've seen Bob's analysis of Medeiros and he's right.

This guy won't sit still. Bill's going to be butt-whooped a fifth time, especially if it's at the hands of the Bobs. I know, Thor, I replied.

That's what worries me.
