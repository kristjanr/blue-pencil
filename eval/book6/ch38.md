Glad of any excuse for a break, I opened it, then I frowned. It was from a friend. Really?

Had I just received 24th century spam? Reluctantly, I read further. Bill, you may be tempted to put the Dutchman project on the back burner because of the war.

Don't. It's important. It took less than a micro to realize that this was probably Thoth kibitzing again.

On the one hand, that was incredibly annoying and patronizing. On the other hand, he hadn't steered me wrong yet. Well, except when he was plotting his escape, and I could understand that.

I looked back at the email headers. No routing or return address, of course, but there was no doubt in my mind who this was from. I stared at the email for several more mils, then forwarded it to Garfield with no comment, save a string of question marks.
