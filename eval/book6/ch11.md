I received a call from Greg. Greg, the Bob who had gone off to find out why he disappeared. I invited him over and he appeared in my VR.

Hi, Greg, find anything?

Unfortunately, yes, Bill. A debris field. I sat up straight, switched off all non-essentials in my VR and gave him my full attention.

Was it identifiable as a heaven vessel?

Yes. I can't tell definitively if it was me because I couldn't find any identification markings, but it looks like the right hull version. And the hull's contents, such as I've been able to find, are consistent with what I was carrying.

And the flight path matches.

Could it have been an asteroid strike or something? Actually, Bill, based on the pattern of warping and melting, I think the dead man went off. I stared into space, nonplussed.

That meant that either Greg had been damaged to the point where his dead man switch was triggered, or he'd done it himself. The dead man switch had been added to our standard ship design during the Others' War to prevent them from taking one of us apart like they had with the Chinese space probe. These days it was a magnetic bottle full of antimatter, since Casimir Power Systems didn't fail as spectacularly as the old fusion reactors.

Any other indications? Greg looked to the side for a moment, plainly upset. I checked the debris field for adhesions or foreign matter of any kind.

An asteroid strike would have left pieces of asteroid either on the debris or floating around with it. I found none. What I did find?

Greg hesitated, then made eye contact. It was traces of the byproducts from the IMX explosive used in standard missiles. I was attacked, Bill.

Attacked and destroyed. My worst fears were confirmed. Someone had attacked a Bob.

But why? I shook my head. Didn't matter.

Greg, thanks for bringing this to my attention. I'm going to implement some policies. Bobiverse wide.

Thanks, Bill. I'll wait to hear, he said, then disappeared. I took some time to think

things through, then composed a message for the general channel. Attention all Bobs. A Heaven vessel

was attacked and destroyed by beings unknown sometime in the last few weeks, in the BACATAR system.

Evidence indicates standard military missiles were used. First, please check with any Bobs that you have regular contact with.

We need to find out if anyone is missing. Second, maintain continuous contact, if at all possible, or frequent check-ins otherwise.

Ultima Thule has the capability to record a continuous audio Video Feed. Bill.

I groaned as I realized this was really going to stress my storage capacity. Time to start upgrading.
