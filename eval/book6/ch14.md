September 2347. Bobs in general had taken my warning seriously, and Ultima Thule was getting a great stress test of its comms system.

I'd also received a number of reports of Bobs that were out of contact. Follow-ups had eliminated most of them, but we were left with four names, Phil, Sauron, Kevin, and Darkside, and the associated metadata identifications.

I put out a general message with this information and asked that these Bobs report in, or anyone who had seen them recently, report their last known position. I didn't have

Hopes. Greg's experience, plus what we knew of the other four's movements, told me that these Bobs would have been targeted specifically because they were alone and outside the normal space lanes. I was beginning to suspect that someone was targeting Bobs, and it wasn't casual or accidental.

Then I had an even worse thought. I'd been concentrating on missing Bobs for obvious reasons, but what if other vessels were also going missing? There weren't very many ex-human replicants flying spaceships, but human-occupied ships had multiplied exponentially since the implementation of WormNet.

There were undoubtedly many opportunities for other ships to be attacked.

I quickly sent out queries to all the Bob contacts in the 13 colonies and received acts immediately. It would take days to get the information from the human agencies, assuming the governments cooperated at all. Bobs were still not the humans' best buds.

I texted Will with a summary and received an invitation. Will's basic motif was a medieval castle, but nothing else was compatible with the structure. He was sitting with Spike in his lap in one of those modern gaming chairs with dozens of available adjustments.

It was what you get when you take a lazy boy to ridiculous lengths. I nodded to him, then looked around.

Will's VR changed often, but usually had a backdrop of one of the planets he'd discovered on his explorations. Today was no exception. I gazed out through a picture window at a bizarre landscape.

Huge, roughly conical plants stood out over a solid carpet of low, floppy, bush-like things. The foliage came in all colors and shades, producing a quilted effect. In the background, an actual volcano was simmering.

Is this quilt? I asked, frowning. No.

Quilt has a very evolved ecosystem, Will replied, then emphasized. Very evolved. This planet is probably in its first 50 million years of having land plants.

It doesn't even have trees yet. No lignin, which is why those conical thingies. I grunted, sort of interested, but not willing to be derailed.

So the stuff I texted you about? Yeah. Interesting.

Bob's getting taken out seemingly at random. Any non-Bob events? I haven't found any yet, but I don't really expect to start getting responses from human agencies for at least a few more days.

But this is worrying enough, Will. Sure, okay. The question is, are Bobs being specifically targeted, or is it just any ship out on its own?

Well, there's the $64,000 question, I paused, thinking it through. All the disappearances are happening around the edges of human space. Of course, five isn't much of a data set either.

Right, Will replied. So either someone is targeting Bobs, or they're just targeting anyone. Either they're working the edges of known space, or that's just the events we've found so far, and either this is for a specific

nefarious purpose like intelligence gathering, or it's just opportunistic predation for fun. He grinned at me as he finished the sentence. I glared back at him.

Sarcasm detected and screw you. Okay, I get the point. We don't have enough information to work with yet.

Will shrugged, continuing to smile. We don't even know if it's just happening in the UFS, for all we know. The Bobs and the PGF exploration group are getting picked off too.

True. We don't have communications with them and they don't have communications with each other except by store and forward with that buoy setup. Speaking of, I'll send

a summary to the buoy in the Malacca system and the next group member to return for any reason. We'll pick it up and then update other buoys on their travels. Will nodded.

I don't want to minimize your concerns, Bill. Bob's getting destroyed is serious business, but until we can establish what's even going on, there's not much we can do directly. Yeah, got it.

I guess I just needed to hear it from someone else. I understand. We all go through that.

Want to stay for a coffee? Sure. I have a few mils.

I looked around the room. But no gaming chairs. Will laughed as a lazy boy appeared behind me with a coffee on a side table.

Much better.
