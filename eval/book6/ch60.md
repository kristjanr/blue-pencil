I keep thinking something's off. But really, if the PGF civilizations just connected to the network one species at a time, I think this is what you'd end up with. I nodded.

And without replicants to run the von Neumann probes and only sublight drives to get around with. And yet, you'd think there would still be exploration. Well, there was, I pointed out.
