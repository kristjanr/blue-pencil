February 2348. Garfield poked at the control board a few times then gave me a grin. We are ready for test.

Target drones? We have 12 targets out there ranging from 2 light minutes to 3 light hours away. The warp torpedoes are programmed to get close by dead reckoning then drop out of warp, do a SUDAR sweep, and jump in close.

The targets will start evasive maneuvers as soon as they feel the SUDAR scan. Well then, let's do it. Garfield hit the execute button and the status windows showed 12 warp torpedoes disappear from the staging area.

We immediately started getting telemetry from observation drones as well as the torpedoes and targets. First two came out right on top of the targets. Garfield intoned.

No corrective jump required. Clean kills. We continued watching.

Every few mils, another target was reported as neutralized. The last couple of targets, though, took several extra jumps to be taken out. When the last one was gone, I said, any idea what was going on there at the end?

It looks like the farther out the targets were, the more error on the warp jump. The last two torpedoes had to jump three times to get close enough. They each over-jumped at least once.

Not good when your opponent is sentient and unfriendly. Can we compensate? Garfield thought for a moment.

I think if we have the torpedoes always jump short by 100 milliseconds, then scan and do a precision jump, we can get rid of the issues. No way Medeiros will be able to react correctly to a bogey showing up for a few mils, then disappearing.

Fair enough, how long to implement? A few minutes, Bill, it's just software. Excellent, I'm going to contact everyone who is supplying us with autofactory time and alert them to a new pattern coming in.

We're going to go 100% torpedoes.
