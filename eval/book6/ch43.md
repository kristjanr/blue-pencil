Bill, Ultima Thule, January 2348 I was working on the FTL project in a desultory manner, unable to concentrate on items related to the invasion, but at the same time unable to concentrate on anything else. Then I received a text from Bob.

It was a link to a news feed, to which Bob had added the caption, Jeebus H. Christopher Cross on a crutch.

Well, if nothing else, the caption caught my interest. I pulled up the feed and opened it in a separate window. Then I stared, my eyes goggling

Oh, you have got to be kidding, I muttered The figure on the screen was very familiar Major Ernesto Medeiros, the lunatic replicant from the Brazilian von Neumann probe

I'd never personally met him, not as Bill anyway, but I remembered the first face-off as Bob won in Epsilon Eridani more than 200 years ago Medeiros had actually taken the time to have a video conversation with Bob He'd been a pompous martinet then, and based on what I was hearing now, nothing had changed

I rewound the feed to the beginning to get the whole story I am Major Ernesto Medeiros of the Brazilian Empire My mission was originally to find colony targets for the Empire

Now my mission is to avenge the destruction of the Empire on Old Earth and to re-establish it among the stars, on top of the ashes of the rest of humanity if necessary Any who are willing to accept and bow to the authority of the Empire will be spared

The first to do so will be welcomed into the empire. The last holdouts will be destroyed utterly. Only those of Brazilian descent will be considered citizens and able to serve in government.

If you meet this requirement, step forward with proof of your claim and a place will be made for you. To the rest of humanity, you exist and will continue to exist on my suffrage. And my patience grows short.

I have been preparing for this day for more than 200 years. I have been studying your governments, your military, your society. I know all about your capabilities and you know nothing of mine.

Except this, I do not expect to lose or I would not be here today. To Bob Johansson and the rest of the Bobiverse, you are anathema. You were fortunate in the past but your luck is at an end.

You are nothing and less than nothing and you will be consigned to the dust heap of history. There will be no mercy, no negotiation. You will be obliterated just as I have already destroyed several of your fellows.

Easily. Casually.

Medeiros glared into the camera for several seconds. Then his image was replaced with what I assumed was the Brazilian Empire flag from Earth.

In the middle of my bristling disbelief, I got a ping from Bob, followed immediately by him popping in.

So, interesting times, he said and plunked himself down in one of the available deck chairs. He gestured to Jeeves with a drinking motion, then continued. Materos has delivered a written and far more detailed ultimatum to go along with his speech, or screed, or rant, or whatever you prefer.

He accepted a coffee from Jeeves. He has control of the high ground in Arcadia, New Babylon, and Purgatory, at least from the point of view of a military threat. He's knocked out all the defenders and could pound the planets into extinction, a point that he wasn't shy about making.

Bob sighed and made a helpless gesture into the air. He's expanded on what he said about government. He wants to make the colonies part of the new Brazil.

He's called for persons of Brazilian descent to step forward and take the reins of government. And speaking of which, he wants the colonies to convert to a parliamentary monarchy. Bob paused and looked at me expectantly.

I turned my head and frowned in thought. But the Brazilian empire in the 22nd century wasn't a monarchy. Or a parliamentary democracy.

Or even a democracy. No, but the empire of Brazil in the 19th century was. I stared at Bob.

Excuse me? Look, I know you have my memories of the first encounter with this kook, but I think I still have a more visceral feel for it. Something is off.

This Medeiros is every bit as pompous as the original version, but there's something else as well. There's an edge or a warp or something. Obsession.

Maybe with his mission, maybe with finally defeating the Bobs. He has lost against us four times now, you know. Honestly, Bill, this feels like some kind of high-functioning dementia.

And he's conflating his version of the Brazilian Empire with a 19th-century version of the Empire of Brazil. Bob gestured at the window, still frozen with the flag displayed. That, for instance, is not the flag of the 22nd century Brazilian empire.

It's the 19th century version.

Well, okay. But he's also hearkening back to some of the social structures of the 19th century.

Again, the pause, the expectant look. I'm not going to like this, am I? Nope.

Rolling back women's rights, reintroduction of slavery, although he doesn't come out and call it that, loss of citizenship protections for non-whites. Oh, and an end to replication, destruction of all

Arcologies, and any extant replicants to be declared criminals and destroyed on sight, especially Bob's. He makes a specific point of that. I pinched the bridge of my nose.

Oy vey. Then, does he want one king or one king per world? Bob rolled his eyes.

Who cares? Regardless, he's going to have to at least drop some bombs. People won't just fold on this.

Oh, and the best part, there's more? Bob nodded. He's ceased all attacks in those three systems, but he's warned the rest of the UFS to stay away and to treat the three colonies as a

Sovereign Power, or else. Or else what? Oh, just the usual huffing and puffing, just leave it as or else.

Is he recalling his ships from other systems? There's no indication he intends to stop his assault on other systems, but Medeiros doesn't have access to worm nets since we deflated all the portals, and I guess he's

realistic enough not to demand it, so any ships in Concord's systems are effectively out of play.

Bob hesitated. This tells me that his assets are fairly evenly distributed since he wouldn't have known in advance which systems would fall first.

I leaned back and gazed at the ceiling for a moment.

So, we have to knock back his attacks, then clean up his lurking armies before we can risk taking back the ones he's conquered, and with the whole Oort cloud to hide in, each system could take years. About sums it up.

Nice planning on his part. That doesn't seem to jibe well with dementia. I said he was crackers, not

incompetent, Bob replied. Also, it's not like biological dementia. Maybe I should have said psychosis.

Okay, I think I have to call a meeting. I have some tech I've been working on, but we may need to rush it to production. Got it.

Bob stood. Let me know when. But let's not leave it too long.
