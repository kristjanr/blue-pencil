The Medeiros War was over, and I was looking forward to getting back to my regular headaches. We'd gotten nothing done on the mover plate manufacturing, although Bobby's system looked rich enough to qualify as a major source. It would reduce the system's value as a colony target, of course, but I wasn't sure if that was still the critical issue that it used to be.

The Nostromo was still doing a beeline through the cosmos, identifying colony targets as it went Will had more than two dozen now that had just been quietly added to inventory Meanwhile, the O'Neill Corporation had officially launched the A.C.

Clark and had three other megastructures under construction The Clark had a population capacity of 600,000 or so Not huge by planetary standards, but O'Neill could pump more megastructures out assembly line fashion

They wouldn't be immediately heading out of town, though The news about warp drives was resulting in some reformulations of plans For one thing, the corporation could fly a wormhole kernel out a hundred light years in a little more than two years, grow the kernel, then push the Clark through the wormhole.

Long-distance travel would be drastically redefined, and the board was reconsidering final destinations in light of the suddenly increased reach.

Times just kept getting more interesting.

With a feeling of finally getting a chance to breathe, I transferred to my mani, settled in my favorite deck chair on the patio, took a deep breath and relaxed. And the phone rang.

Again, not literally. I got an email with an attention flag. Sighing, I opened it, expecting something from Will or Bob concerning the colonial governments.

Did I mention that despite the Bobiverse having essentially saved the day, the USF membership had roundly condemned our interference? Sometimes I really hated humans. Not as often as Will, but sometimes.

I frowned looking at the email in the floating window. From a friend. Again?

Seriously, couldn't he just sign it, Thoth? Bill, just a suggestion for you. When creating wormhole pairs, if you charge the initial kernel with the energy phased per the attached diagram, you will get an interesting effect.

Okay. This was a little different. I wasn't being asked to do or not do something that would create moral quandaries, just an engineering suggestion.

I looked at the attached diagram. It was a negative energy waveguide setup, of the kind we use to create the wormholes, but with a few novel tweaks. It appeared I would have to build the device to find out what interesting effect I would get.
