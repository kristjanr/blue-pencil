The overhead dome was huge, literally a mile in diameter and more than a quarter mile high at the center. I gazed upward in awe.

Without using telescopic vision, I couldn't make out any detail on the inside surface. Of course, it wasn't a rigid dome. We had a 1.2 bar atmosphere inside, which, with the 0.75 bar pressure outside on the surface of Valhalla, meant air pressure literally did all the heavy lifting.

I couldn't help grinning. Another crazy idea was going to pay off big time. We already had reservations for the XO Adventure Park pre-booked out to 18 months, with the park still 7 months from opening.

Apparently, humans liked the idea of being able to fly, and they'd be able to rent a dranny by the hour or by the day, or a quinny if they preferred an aquatic adventure. The park had a series of rivers and small lakes at ground level with artificial currents for that end of things.

When the park was completed, hologram projectors would replicate the view as seen on Jabberwocky. With the higher air pressure in the dome and lower gravity of Valhalla, drannies would be able to fly here. I was so tired of making money.

Bridget just rolled her eyes at me when I tried to low-key humble brag, but this was my superpower. I brought my attention back to ground level as the foreman walked up, and I turned to acknowledge him.

Some issues with those component deliveries we talked about, Mr. Johansson, but by and large were on schedule.

Send me the details on those, Harlan, I'll see if I can help things along. He nodded, expression neutral, and touched the brim of his hard hat in a sort of salute before marching off. No doubt I'd taken him away from what he considered his real work, and I could sympathize.

I'd always hated it when upper management stuck their noses in, and now I was that upper management. The irony burned. When his report arrived, I'd see if I had any relationships with the companies in question through which I could apply some pressure.

Hell, I was probably a major shareholder in at least some of them. A schedule alert flashed in my heads up. Meeting with the new home chancellor.

Ugh. Another reminder that being dead didn't mean rest in peace. The introduction of Hueys had clashed with the new home administration's policies on every possible level when they were already having fits about manis.

Although they didn't have the clout to completely ban the technology, they were being as obstructionist as possible. under the guise of reasonable caution. This would not be pleasant.

I instructed my manny to go park itself and disconnected, then connected to my personal manny on Newholm. For instructions to local staff, it had

already been driven to the Newholm Central Government Building and was waiting in the passenger seat of the traxi. I sat up, tapped the door, and exited the traxi, then headed for the building entrance, where I

could see there was someone waiting for me. I was hustled through security and ushered into Chancellor Rickmanhorst's office with minimal delay.

While it was normal political maneuvering to keep people waiting in order to establish dominance, the equation changed when they wanted something from me. Plus, the last time they'd tried that, I'd simply left after telling them to reschedule.

We shook hands, Horst gestured to a seat, and he leaned back in his expensive office chair. So, Mr. Johansson, I won't waste your time with a long-winded screed.

You understand the problem we are facing? I understand that you consider it a problem, Chancellor. I don't understand why, though.

Our citizens are understandably concerned that the person next to them may not, in fact, be a person, but an unpredictable and potentially dangerous robotic device. They feel the government is failing in its

duty by not having a plan to deal with this issue. Chancellor, your planet is the only one where this is a problem.

It's also the only one where there's a dedicated, concerted, government-funded social media blitz specifically designed to target and magnify this fear. Perhaps an easier and cheaper solution

would be to stop trying to convince people that they're in some kind of danger. You may be confusing cause and effect, Mr.

Johansson. What the citizens say on social media is a reflection of their feelings, not the cause of them.

I rolled my eyes and didn't care in the slightest if the Chancellor saw it. Sure, it's a grassroots reaction. Nothing at all to do with the millions of PAMs your government is spending

on focus groups, marketing firms, and bot farms. I leaned forward. In this day and age, it is very

difficult to completely obscure the money trail, Chancellor. Please don't insult me. Horst flushed

and his lips grew thin. He paused, then replied. You may believe what you wish to believe, Mr.

Johansson. However, if you want to continue to do business in the new home system, any kind of business, you are going to have to be receptive to our concerns. We don't ask for much, really, just some way of identifying a Huey or Manny versus a real human being.

Like an H on the forehead, I said sarcastically. That would certainly be acceptable. I didn't.

I stopped myself.

I had intended the suggestion, ironically, but the Chancellor didn't get the reference. Of course, why would he? Red Dwarf was coming up on 400 years old.

But that being the case, this offered some amusing possibilities.

You understand, this would be done only on Newholm. Horst smiled and spread his hand, sensing a victory. That is of no concern to me, sir.

I am the Chancellor for Newholm, not for all of the UFS. We chatted for a few more minutes, the tension having somewhat abated. Then I bid him goodbye, called a traxi, and headed for the curb.

You're serious, Bridget said. An H on the forehead. And you agreed to this?

Yes, and it'll go into effect immediately. I'm certain the Chancellor will be very public about having extracted this concession from us. It's from an old TV show from when I was bio.

No reason you or anyone else would recognize the reference. In fact, I continued, I may be able to make use of that. A little bit of payback, you might say.

Bridget smiled. I have no idea what you're talking about, but you are so hot when you're being evil.
