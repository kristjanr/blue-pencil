I set my heading for the wormhole portal, excitement making me almost vibrate.

I'd received the SCUT announcement about WormNet a few years ago, and I was overjoyed to realize that I would end up near one of the wormhole-connected systems.

There appeared to be some secrecy about exactly why that chain of stellar systems had been created, though. Anytime I asked questions, the answers were, well, evasive. Which was odd for a bunch of bobs.

But I figured there had to be a good reason. And regardless, I was about to find myself able to re-enter human space. Physically, I mean, not just in VR.

I was seriously going to talk to Bill about getting one of the new Titan-class hulls. I transited the wormhole without issue. Hull sensors indicated a little bit of inward pressure, which was expected, according to the docs.

I started a course correction to, warning, multiple hull breaches, power system failing, cooling system failing. Yuppie, what just happened? What hit us?

No data. SUDDAR offline. I was going to have to call for help.

Had I materialized in the middle of an asteroid clump? I activated SCUT. Or tried to, it was down too.

Another impact. All hull sensors went dead. I activated visuals and scanned the area.

A half dozen ships were approaching me from multiple vectors. They didn't look familiar in any specific way, but they gave the sense of being human rather than alien. The lines, architecture, technical design were all comfortably recognizable.

No insignia or identification numbers, though. Someone was ambushing me. Then I got a message.

Text, not audio. Surrender or be destroyed. This is not a negotiation.

Surrender? And be put up on a rack and slowly taken apart? Or even worse, hacked?

I'd sent a differential update to Ultima Thule a couple of days ago, so I'd be a little out of date, but the alternative was literally a fate worse than death. Of course, if Bill didn't notice that I'd stopped sending backups, I might never be restored. That would be death, in the most real sense.

Still, there really was no choice. I steeled myself, then said, Guppy, blow the dead man.

Alert! Controller has given destruct order. Requirements for self-destruct protocol have been met.

Anti-matter containment disengaged.
