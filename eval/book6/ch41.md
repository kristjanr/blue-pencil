After the ancients' meeting I asked Will to hang around for a bit. Garfield gave me a perplexed look but shrugged and popped out with the rest. I pulled up a video and telemetry file that Bob had emailed me before our meeting, moved it to a window and showed it to Will.

This is the recording of the PAV battle against the invaders. This battle was surprisingly one-sided. Are the PAV that good?

Or is this mystery attacker that incompetent? It's all three. I only gave two options.

Will chuckled. Yeah, but there's three reasons. One, the attacking Navy really does seem to be operating in a stereotypical and inflexible manner.

I noticed about 30 seconds into the battle that they were repeating strategies without any randomization. Apparently the PAV picked up on that too. Interesting.

And it could spawn a whole discussion about whether we're dealing with sentience or some version of AMIs, but continue. Two, the Pav really are that good, or at least that fearless. They seem to have an aspect of kamikaze tactics that I don't think the opposition was prepared for.

It's not something humans would embrace until things had become desperate, as with the Japanese in World War II. Certainly not a tactic of first choice, except with the Pav, it is. Sounds Klingon.

More like Jem'Hadar, I think. Okay, anyway. And three, well, the Pav outnumbered the opposition, so I don't think the losses they inflicted are unreasonable, given that.

So there's nothing weird going on. We don't have to worry about the Pav being super soldiers. Will bobbed his head.

That wouldn't go that far. They are pretty fierce. Hey, here's an idea.

Let's just not get into a situation where it matters. He hesitated. Then his avatar froze for a moment.

When he unfroze, he said, I just got an update, Bill. Another wave of attackers appeared out of nowhere in the Kashmir and Las Cañadas systems and started pushing the defenders back.

The Pav are starting to lose units simply because they're outnumbered. Flying in from somewhere else? Don't know.

There's no indication of them having access to a wormhole. They might have just been gathered in the outer system. The bottom line, though, is that we've just about lost all the headway that the Pav gained.

I grimaced. Not a good start. They seem to have a large, and I mean huge, supply of backups.

Will mused. Why? Why not just swoop in with the whole squad?

Will shrugged. Psychological? Every time we think we're beating them back, they just produce more troops?

It would get discouraging after a while. Or maybe this way we use up all our military assets, I replied. Otherwise we'd just surrender.

Dunno, Bill. Maybe we're dealing with aliens and alien motivations. But very human tech levels?

I'm beginning to lean toward the AMI theory.

Starfleet? I don't... I stopped and considered.

You know, at least part of Starfleet is legitimately nutso based on my discussions with Charles. It's not the worst theory. And maybe they're not trying to defeat the colonies, just so more dissension.

Will shook his head and stood. Speculation is pointless until we get more info and meanwhile I have an Earth-resettlement to oversee. Talk later.
