Bill, being a Bob, had given us a comprehensive instruction manual, and we, also being Bobs, actually read it. Original Bob never had an ego issue with reading documentation, and in fact hated getting things wrong and having to redo them.

We hadn't drifted from that attitude, so we were following the instructions point by point.

Something that hadn't really come up back in Epsilon Eridani was that Titan-class vessels came with three times the roamer and drone population, including something that I had never seen before, a shape-changing roamer. Romer.

It was marked as experimental and use at own risk, but both Goku and I ended up wasting far too much time playing with it. It was spherical, about the size of a softball when in standby mode, and had pre-programmed shapes set up but could be molded to anything we wanted. It was basically a T-1000 Terminator in miniature, and without the homicidal tendencies, hopefully.

I wasn't sure how useful it would be, though, since a shape-shifting Romer didn't have the structural strength of a real one, or some of the tools, but we were determined that we would find a use for it. Anyway, the large roamer population was a good thing because the assembly of the portal satellites into a negative energy forcing system was a major project. I had visions of roamers running around with little sweat droplets radiating out of them.

Goku suggested they might try to unionize halfway through.

It took two weeks to bring it all together, but finally we had what we needed, a truly huge negative energy generator. The idea was to expand the wormhole mouth at this end and inject enough negative energy to force open the other end. Bill was fairly sure we could do it with this setup, but he wasn't sure of the actual energy requirements.
