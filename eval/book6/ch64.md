I was sitting in my VR patio, staring into space and trying to second-guess Medeiros when Garfield popped in unannounced. Another announcement from Medeiros, he said without preamble. I rolled my eyes but pulled up a window and started the transmission from the beginning.

A small demonstration of my ability and intent, as promised, Medeiros said, then faded out to be replaced with the Brazilian flag. I frowned. That was it?

Sorry, Bill, I came over as soon as he started transmitting. I guess that's all there was. So, what demonstration?

I was interrupted by a text from Gordon in the Great River System.

Fifth planet of system has been destroyed. Crust totally churned. Molten magma splashing up to 50 kilometers in height.

Suspect Medeiros' attack.

Gordon's email came with a video link, so I opened another window.

It was certainly one of the most awesome and terrifying things I'd ever seen. The fifth planet, which I think had been named Galvlo I, had essentially been shattered, like an eggshell, over the entire globe The red glow of internal magma showed through cracks everywhere, and the surface appeared blurry, which was probably due to it being spread over up to 50 kilometers of altitude

I didn't believe the planet had been inhabited, but if it had, everyone would certainly be dead I popped over into Gordon's VR Any idea what happened?

I'm just getting telemetry now, he replied he paused and his expression became fearful. Wow, it looks like the planet was hit by tens of thousands of metallic spheres all approaching from different directions.

I gawked. Busters. Medeiros didn't need mover plates to launch asteroids.

In fact, this was a better tactic from his point of view. We wouldn't have a single large mass to intercept. We'd have thousands of small ones.

Busters were just steel balls with a drive and an AMI controller strapped on. I'd designed them. Well, Bob, one had designed them for ramming and they were, like sharks,

Darks, perfectly adapted to their niche. Son of a bitch, I muttered, time for a moot. The crowd was loud and argumentative.

The news about Galvlo, I, had precipitated a fair amount of chaos and I pretty much gave up on getting order back, at least for the moment. Several blats of the air horn had been totally ineffective. So I waited, crossing my arms, trying my best to look both bored and impatient, and for a wonder it appeared to work.

The crowd noise died down gradually and the hall became quiet, except for the occasional titter of amusement Okay, so, anything constructive? I asked

Hunt Medeiros down and wipe him out, someone yelled While I don't disagree, I replied, we don't have the assets to do that quickly enough to prevent him from destroying at least some of the colonies Billions of lives

Do what he says? Someone else called out and was met with a chorus of boos I made a calm down motion

I know it's not a popular option, but it should at least be discussed, I said to the crowd. The problem is that's not a decision we would have a say in.

If any colony decides to go that way, it'll happen whether or not we approve. So while it's a possibility, it's not something we can act on.

Give him what he wants, what he really, really wants, called out a familiar voice. I looked through the crowd and spotted

Bobby. We calmly waited out the booze, and as soon as they died down, I asked, and what does he really, really want.

Brazil. The Brazilian Empire. Or at least a Brazilian state.

How is that different from surrendering? Someone yelled.

She turned in the general direction of the voice. We give him a Brazilian state, but our version.

He wants a Brazilian state run by people of Brazilian descent? Well, we have all the Brazilians.

She turned until she spotted Will in the crowd. Don't we, Will? Will was taken aback at being

singled out like that. He sputtered for a moment, then the light apparently dawned. Yes, he said.

Yes, we do, Don't We? Okay, this is the closest we've gotten to a real plan, I announced.

Moot adjourned. The beer's on me. Ancients to my VR.

And before the crowd would do more than begin to protest, I'd popped back into my VR. I was followed in close order by Will, Bob, Howard, Bridget, Garfield, and Bobby. Bridget grinned at Bobby and deliberately parked herself next to her.

Howard looked momentarily crestfallen, then shrugged and pulled up a lawn chair. Guppy chose that moment to appear. 4,387 connection attempts to personal VR.

This far exceeds capacity. Instructions? I growled and muttered.

Everyone wants to kebbets. Then louder. Reject all, Guppy, politely but firmly.

I can do firmly, Guppy said and disappeared to general laughter. So, Bobby, I said, tell us more. She nodded toward Will.

I'm sure Will has it figured out by now. He has something like 30,000 people repopulating Earth. More, Will interjected.

Once the project became a thing, a crap ton more people applied to join.

We're well over 100,000 people now, and we've had to create a full-time department just to process applications.

And some of them are of Brazilian descent, Bobby added. In fact, if the only criterion is being able to show a family tree that places one or more of your ancestors in Brazil, and you're willing to go back far enough, I think most humans alive today would qualify.

It's not like the population has been genetically isolated. Now Will was grinning. So we get a bunch of people with proper papers together.

Have them declare themselves a nation, I added. Lay claim to their ancestral lands, Garfield chimed in. We all stopped and frowned at him.

What? Come on, it's icing on the cake. Medeiros won't be able to resist it.

Not the worst idea, Will muttered, although we'd been aiming for more of a one-Earth-united vibe, and it is a good settlement target.

And then the newly minted nation of Brazil orders Medeiros to stand down, Bobby finished. Yeah, about that, Howard said. It's a gamble, Bobby admitted.

Medeiros might just laugh in our faces. I mean, it is pretty transparent, but on the other hand, we're theoretically giving him what he wants. With his ego, he might choose to interpret it as capitulation rather than deception.

He'll still be suspicious, but he'll believe he can outsmart anything we might try to pull. After a moment's thought, I added, Also, there's the matter of his imperatives.

If we can spin this so that it plays to his built-in goals, he might literally not be able to resist.

Will shook his head slowly. Seriously, what do we have to lose? Everyone started grinning as the idea took hold.

Do we need anything else? I asked. Well, we need a government, Will replied.

I can go through the records and pull up some people who would fit. And we need a palace, Garfield added. Say what?

said Bob. Bobby answered. Garfield's right.
