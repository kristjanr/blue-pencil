Why this is a separate page from the atlas

  • It needs the database, not the export. reports — the hops, with sender, channel and arrival — is not in bp export, so the shareable atlas cannot draw a path at all.
  • It needs the real function. Re-implementing light-lag, channel windows and fork inheritance in the browser would put two copies of the epistemic rules in play that can silently disagree. This calls KnowledgeGraph.earliest_knowledge and draws what it returns.
  • It needs the book text. The export carries no prose and no citation quotes by design, so the question of whether a quoted line supports its claim can only be asked here, where the corpus is already local.
  • A hollow ring is a computed arrival — derived from the series' space model. A filled one was stated in the text. A checker that cannot tell those apart is guessing.
