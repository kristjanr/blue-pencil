A Note on This Edition

This is a personal, non-commercial transcript, produced by the owner of the audiobook from the Audible narration for private reading. It is not the publisher’s text.

The words were transcribed from the audio and have since been edited; chapter breaks, paragraphing, and headings were reconstructed algorithmically, so both will differ in places from the published edition. Known gaps and uncertain passages are listed in the accompanying transcription notes.

The recording begins part-way through the epigraph, so none of the pages before it were captured. The titles list, title page, copyright page, and dedication are reconstructed from the retail editions of books 1–5 and from the credits read at the end of the recording, and the author’s contact details, About the Author, and About the Publisher pages are taken from the retail edition of book 5; the epigraph’s first sentence is the standard wording of a quotation whose second sentence is the first thing the narration preserves. Books 4 and 5 carry the dedication printed here word for word, but whether book 6 repeats it is not something the audio can settle.
